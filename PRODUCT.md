# PRODUCT.md

## 1\. The job

When I am unsure on how to prepare my kid (15-18 yr old) for a career in 2030s, I want to understand how the future jobs will look like directly, so that I can start preparing my kid in a informed manner

Who, by situation (not age or city): A parent of a child (15-18 yr old) who is actively exploring the best way to prepare their child for future workforce (2030s)

Today they hire:

There is no standardized structured approach today. Parents are using a combination of multiple steps as listed below.
1. The parent evaluate the latest trend for current professions and plan education for their kids which may result in high paying salary. For example, in current age of AI, parents are looking to enroll kids in an undergraduate program in AI or Data science because that is earning top dollars currently. 
2. The parents are discussing the potential career options with kid as well as within their friends and family circle.
3. Kid's aspiration are either influenced by their parents, friends or family members. A lot of them are able to visualize how a particular career will look like.
4. Kid's have become power users of social media. There are getting inspiration and trends from content posted on social media channels by young people from across the globe. 
5. Some parents are consulting with career counsellors especially when the kid does not show interest in science post Class 10.
6. The counsellors are making kid write a psychometric test using old techniques and comes out with a strength & competency scorecard.
7. Based on the competency store they are predicting the top 3 strength areas for e.g. Good with number, excellent communications, etc
8. The recommendation careers are existing career options matching to top 3 strength area.
9. Parents are making decision based on these recommendation but are still not very confident about the kid's future.
10. They enroll the kid in entrance exam coaching sometime without even factoring on kid's interest
11. Parent focus is primarily limited to get the kids into a college of good pedigree at undergraduate level. They hope the college and kid's cohort will prepare the kid for future career.
12. Some parents plan to send kids for undergraduate program in western countries (Canada, Australia, NZ, Europe) as a proxy to more market aligned education and job opportunities post education.
13. In case the kid is not able to make it to top college in India, they enroll the kid to the best available college option and hope for the best!
14. Parents are budgeting an expense of 10-20 lakhs including coaching, course free, boarding and lodging.
15. Parents are also putting kids in multiple activity classes ranging from public speaking, AI hackathon, Robotics classes, etc along with schools.

## 2\. The switch

What they'd fire: Counselling option. It gives an assessment of the kid's of current strengths and competencies. It does not give them an actionable roadmap to prepare them for future.

Push (what's wrong with today): They are still confused about the best way to prepare their kids for the future.
Pull (what your product promises): It will help them visualize future roles based on theirs's kids aspiration, identify the skills needed, assess where there kid stand today and get an actionable milestone for next 12 months. In future releases, product will help them track as well as build some of these skills. 
Anxiety (what worries them about switching): Fear of choosing a wrong career path for their kid
Habit (what keeps them where they are): Lack of alternatives. They are following the herd mentality.

The one worry onboarding must remove: Get me the most appropriate futuristic career (max 3) options based on my kid's aspiration and my intent.
How I know (what they did, not what they said): They were circumspect of the career choices they made for their kids.

## 3\. The core flow

The story: \[The parent wants to know the most appropriate futuristic career (max 3) options based on their kid's aspiration with the required skills so that can start preparing their kids accordingly.]


1. The career options suggested by the career counsellor were mapped to present careers and did not evoke confidence as the future is work is fast changing with rapid evolution in technology especially AI.
2. Parent should be able to enter kids interests in plain text for e.g "Economics, Book writing, food critique."
3. Parent should be able to enter their intent in plain text for e.g "Good Earning potential, Stability, Foreign work location"
3. The AI should read the kid's interest and parent intent to come up with max 3 futuristic career pathways as per major publications/surveys prediction available in internet. Please make sure that AI output is consistent when key words are similar.
4. Each of the career pathways  should be presented with brief job description + why matched + Average yearly salary, Foreign offer possibility. It should show "A day in the life" with details along with a link to see the visual depiction of the same. It should also show the "Skill Map required" detailing the various skills needed (% wise) to perform the selected job for e.g. Finance 40%, Marketing 20% Empathy 25% Communication 15%.
5. When clicking on "A day in the life", it should display a visual/comic representation of that job profile and what skills differentiates in the age of AI.
6. It should also display and activate a "12 months action plan" mapped to "Skill Map required" month wise.
More focus should be on the skills which are ranked more than other skills
7. The user will be able to see end to end flow for first selected career option.
8 For view additional career  option, they must be presented with a message to pay Rs 299/- to proceed. 
9 Once they pay the amount, they should be able to access the full end to end flow for the respective career option.
10 They can go back and change kids class and interests and parent intent anytime. The AI should adjust the recommendation accordingly.

Below steps are nice to have and are to be parked for future development
1. "Skill Buddy" to hand old the parents by tracking and nudging parent to take action if they are falling behind on the action plan.
2. "Skill Buddy" will continue to monitor latest trends powered by AI and share relevant insights.
3. "Skill Buddy" will help searching the relevant service provider/product which can help in skill development within X Km of a PIN code.

Things they do today: \[15] · Things they do with my product: \[3]

What can go wrong:
Step \[3]: \[no matching career pathway] -> what must happen: Suitable messaging to assure the parent that we are continuing working to get a suitable pathway. He shall be notified as soon as their is match!


## 4\. Onboarding

First value (the moment it first does the job for them): As soon as they complete the kid's aspirations and their intent, they should be able to see the proposed career pathways. They should be click on any one of the pathways and see all the related detail.
The worry it removes (from section 2): Getting a futuristic career pathway based on kid's interest and their intent is much more relatable and therefore, actionable.

From opening the link to the first value:

1. The parent receives link and land straight on the ask screen requirements screen.
2. Parent should be able to enter kids interests in plain text for e.g "Economics, Book writing, food critique."
3. Parent should be able to enter their intent in plain text for e.g "Good Earning potential, Stability, Foreign work location"
4. Futuristic career pathways ( max 3 ) along with brief job description  will be displayed.
5. The parent should be able to choose any one of the displayed career path.
6. Once the parent chooses, A day in life with relevant details (for e.g. interest key word matched, closest existing job, annual earning potential (Very High, High, Medium, Low), stability and overseas potential, skill map required) and link to visualization to be presented 
7. Display a tile with the text "The future is not a job title. It is what they can do." to reinforce focus on skilling.
8 Generate a 12 month roadmap with simple actionable and expected outcome mapped to skill map generator.


Login: No login/sign up required on day one.
What we don't ask on day one: profile, sign up, pay to use the product for the detail of first career pathway
What we ask later, and when: After first free career pathway, we ask user to pay followed up by simple but optional sign up so that we can save and display their pass searches.
